<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/0-1-knapsack-problem0945/1?itm_source=geeksforgeeks&itm_medium=article&itm_campaign=practice_card">0 - 1 Knapsack Problem</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Medium</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="user-select: auto;"><p style="user-select: auto;"><span style="font-size: 14pt; user-select: auto;">You are given the weights and values of items, and you need to put these items in a knapsack of capacity <strong style="user-select: auto;">capacity</strong> to achieve the <strong style="user-select: auto;">maximum </strong>total value in the knapsack. Each item is available in <strong style="user-select: auto;">only one</strong> quantity.</span></p>
<p style="user-select: auto;"><span style="font-size: 14pt; user-select: auto;">In other words, you are given two integer arrays <strong style="user-select: auto;"><code style="user-select: auto;">val[]</code></strong> and <strong style="user-select: auto;"><code style="user-select: auto;">wt[]</code></strong>, which represent the values and weights associated with items, respectively. You are also given an integer <strong style="user-select: auto;">capacity</strong>, which represents the knapsack capacity. Your task is to find the maximum sum of values of a subset of <code style="user-select: auto;">val[]</code> such that the sum of the weights of the corresponding subset is less than or equal to <strong style="user-select: auto;">capacity</strong>. You cannot break an item; you must either pick the entire item or leave it (0-1 property).</span></p>

<pre style="user-select: auto;"><span style="font-size: 14pt; user-select: auto;"><strong style="user-select: auto;">Input:</strong> capacity = 3, <code style="user-select: auto;">val[]</code> = [1, 2, 3], <code style="user-select: auto;">wt[]</code> = [4, 5, 6] <br style="user-select: auto;"><strong style="user-select: auto;">Output: </strong>0<br style="user-select: auto;"><strong style="user-select: auto;">Explanation: </strong>Every item has a weight exceeding the knapsack's capacity (3).</span></pre>
