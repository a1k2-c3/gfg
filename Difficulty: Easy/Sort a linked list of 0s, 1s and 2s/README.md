The task is to segregate <strong style="user-select: auto;">0s</strong>, <strong style="user-select: auto;">1s,</strong> and <strong style="user-select: auto;">2s</strong> linked list such that all zeros segregate to the head side, 2s at the end of the linked list, and 1s in the middle of 0s and 2s.</span></p>

Explanation: </strong>All the 0s are segregated to the left end of the linked list, 2s to the right end of the list, and 1s in between.<br style="user-select: auto;"><img src="https://media.geeksforgeeks.org/img-practice/prod/addEditProblem/700028/Web/Other/blobid1_1723665173.png" width="400" height="140" style="user-select: auto;"> </span></pre>
<pre style="user-select: auto;"><span style="font-size: 14pt; user-select: auto;"><strong style="user-select: auto;">Input: </strong>LinkedList: 2-&gt;2-&gt;0-&gt;1
<strong style="user-select: auto;">Output: </strong>0-&gt;1-&gt;2-&gt;2<strong style="user-select: auto;">
Explanation: </strong>After arranging all the 0s,1s and 2s in the given format, the output will be 0 1 2 2.<br style="user-select: auto;"><img src="https://media.geeksforgeeks.org/img-practice/prod/addEditProblem/700028/Web/Other/blobid0_1723665157.png" width="386" height="135" style="user-select: auto;"></span></pre>
